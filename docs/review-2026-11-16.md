## Short answers to likely questions

**What is PANORAMA predicting?** A pancreatic region followed by a PDAC-class
candidate map, uncalibrated scan score and stage-2 class-1 lesion mask/probability.
Our demonstration does not establish an actual patient diagnosis.

**Is 0.9768 a 97.68% chance of cancer?** No. It is the baseline's uncalibrated
maximum candidate score. Calibration and a validated decision threshold are pending.

**Does Dice 0.753 mean 75.3% accuracy?** No. Dice measures spatial agreement of
this one predicted mask and the selected reference. Clinical sensitivity,
specificity and ROC-AUC need a separately labeled patient cohort.

**Why do organs move when sliding?** These are different cross-sections through
one stationary 3D CT volume, not a moving or follow-up scan.

**Where is head/body/tail?** Native coordinates work. PANORAMA does not output
head/body/tail classes. The report accepts a compatible external subregion mask
with provenance/review status; genuine labels and clinical review are pending.

**Did you train this model?** No. We integrated pretrained PANORAMA, preserved
its original five-fold/default-TTA inference, and built checked intake, reporting,
interaction and evaluation around it.
